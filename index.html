The problem

A silent yield killer that farmers can't see.

Indian farmers lose yield because they cannot tell when their flowering crops are under-pollinated, and have no easy way to get bees in time.

20–35%

yield increase reported from managed bee pollination, still unused by most farmers.

₹2,500

per bee box per month. A rent-a-bee market already exists, but farmers have no data to trigger it.

3.4M vs ~200M

managed bee colonies in India today vs. the estimated potential. A huge pollination gap.

Invisible until too late

Flowers look normal. The loss shows up weeks later as poor fruit set, misshapen fruit and lower yield, when nothing can be done.

Bees are declining

Mango, pomegranate, mustard, sunflower, onion seed, cucurbits and many oilseeds need insect pollination, while pesticides, habitat loss and heat stress shrink bee populations.

The fix exists, the demand doesn't

Renting bee boxes works, but few farmers know when or how many hives they need, or whom to trust. Beekeepers sit on idle hives.

Global tools don't fit Indian farms

BeeHero (US) and AgriSound (UK) show growers pay for pollination data, but they rely on in-field hardware for large farms. Smallholders need a phone-only, vernacular, low-cost tool.

Sources: Times of India report on rent-a-bee pollination, as summarised by C4S; BEEKIND / MIT Solve.

How Parag works

Scan. Score. Act.

Parag watches and listens to a flowering field, shows whether pollinator activity is below the benchmark for that crop and stage, and books nearby hives in one tap.

01

Scan

The app guides the farmer to record short video and audio clips at 5 spots in the field during peak bee hours (9–11 am). Works offline.

02

Score

Video-first AI counts bee visits per flower, with buzz audio as a supporting signal, and compares them with published benchmarks for that crop and stage. You get a pollination activity score, a confidence level and an estimate of ₹ yield at risk.

03

Act

If pollination is short, Parag says how many hives you need and for how long. One tap books the nearest verified beekeeper, timed around pesticide sprays. A follow-up scan confirms the improvement.

Technology

Powered by Google AI and Google Cloud.

Serverless, autoscaling and pay-per-use, so Parag can grow from one district to the whole country.

Core brain

Gemini API

Video-first multimodal analysis counts pollinator visits per flower, uses buzz audio as support, and reasons over crop, stage and weather to produce the score, ₹ scenario and hive plan. Flash for scale, Pro for reasoning.

Voice

Gemini Live

Real-time spoken conversations in Marathi, Hindi and other Indian languages, right in the orchard.

On-device

Gemma 3n

Runs on the phone for a preliminary offline estimate and stores clips locally. Keeps costs low at mass scale.

Visuals

Nano Banana

Generates visual report cards and before/after illustrations (flower → healthy fruit) that low-literacy farmers can trust.
